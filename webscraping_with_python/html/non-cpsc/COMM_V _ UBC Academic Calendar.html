Commerce, Faculty of Commerce & Business Administration

COMM_V: Commerce

In general, not all of the prerequisites for Commerce courses are listed. Students must be registered in the same year level as the course they intend to register in. For example, eligibility for Commerce 400-level courses requires a student to have completed second and third-year Commerce, and to be registered in fourth year. Students should refer to the Commerce website or contact the Undergraduate Office regarding course prerequisites and variations from standard program sequences. Additional fees are charged for some courses.

  1. COMM_V 100 (3) Introduction to Business

    An overview of all functional areas of business. Students will be introduced to the computer and internet tools commonly used in business. For non-Commerce students only. Credit will be granted for only one of COMM_V 100 or COMR_V 100. Equivalency: COMR_V 100

  2. COMM_V 101 (3) Business Fundamentals

    The different disciplines of business and their combination in management planning and decision-making. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  3. COMM_V 105 (3) Values, Ethics, and Community

    An introduction to the roles and responsibilities of business at individual, organizational, and societal levels. Students will examine their personal values and lay the foundation for ongoing, active exploration of career paths through which they can achieve their goals. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  4. COMM_V 120 (3) Business Immersion

    A broad introduction to the field of business and how the different disciplines within business interact and combine in business decision-making. For Master of Management - Dual Degree Option students only. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  5. COMM_V 186 (1.5-12) Topics in Business I

    This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  6. COMM_V 190 (3) Introduction to Quantitative Decision Making

    Introduction to decision models in business, including basic optimization, linear programming, probability, decision analysis, random variables, simulation, and solving decision problems using spreadsheet tools. Credit will be granted for only one of COMM_V 190, COMM_V 290, ECON_V 325, ECON_V 327, PSYC_V 366, STAT_V 241, or STAT_V 251. Strongly recommended as corequisite: One of MATH_V 100, MATH_V 102, MATH_V 104, MATH_V 110, MATH_V 120, MATH_V 180, MATH_V 184. Equivalency: COMM_V 290. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  7. COMM_V 191 (3) Application of Statistics in Business

    Methods and applications of statistics in business; data analysis, descriptive regression; data generation; sampling distributions; hypothesis testing; confidence intervals; two sample problems; inference in regression. Credit will be granted for only one of COMM_V 191, BIOL_V 300, COMM_V 291, ECON_V 226, ECON_V 325, ECON_V 327, EPSE_V 482, FRST_V 231, GEOG_V 374, KIN_V 371, PSYC_V 218, PSYC_V 278, PSYC_V 366, POLI_V 380, SOCI_V 328, STAT_V 200, or STAT_V 203. Prerequisite: One of COMM_V 190, COMM_V 290. Equivalency: COMM_V 291, STAT_O 124. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  8. COMM_V 192 (3) Management and Organizational Behaviour

    Behaviour in organizations as it affects people as individuals, their relationships with others, their performance in groups and their effectiveness at work. Credit will be granted for only one of COMM_V 192, COMM_V 292, COMM_V 321, COMM_V 329, or COMR_V 329. Equivalency: COMM_V 292, MGMT_O 230. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  9. COMM_V 196 (3) Business Communications I

This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

For Master of Management - Dual Degree Option students only. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

For Master of Management - Dual Degree Option students only. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  92. COMM_V 439 (3) Business Data Communications

    Basic data communications concepts and technology; local area, wide area, and enterprise networks; the Internet; wireless networking; network development life cycle; network security and management. Credit will be granted for only one of COMM_V 439 or CPSC_V 317, and COMM_V 439 or CPSC_V 417. Prerequisite: COMM_V 335 This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  93. COMM_V 441 (3) Advanced Business Logistics

    Analysis of logistics systems within firms and across supply chains. The formulation of corporate logistics strategies. Corequisite: COMM_V 204. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  94. COMM_V 444 (3) Air Transportation

    An integrative treatment of air transport management including: demand analysis, sales and marketing, globalization trends, aircraft selection and fleet planning; airport economics and management. Prerequisite: One of COMM_V 295, ECON_V 201, ECON_V 301. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  95. COMM_V 445 (3) Maritime and International Transportation

    The characteristics of shipping services and the role of shipping services in the design and management of international logistics systems. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  96. COMM_V 446 (3) Transportation Economics

    Economic characteristics of passenger and freight transportation services; market structure of the transportation industry; economic impact of public regulation and promotion and the role of economic analysis in resolving problems of Canadian policy. Credit will be granted for only one of COMM_V 446 or ECON_V 480. Prerequisite: One of COMM_V 295, ECON_V 201, ECON_V 301, ECON_V 206. Equivalency: ECON_V 480 This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  97. COMM_V 447 (3) Applied Project Management

    Project management methodology based on the practices defined by the Project Management Institute, including planning, execution, monitoring, control, and completion. Prerequisite: One of COMM_V 295, ECON_V 301. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  98. COMM_V 448 (3) Business Logistics and Logistics Services Management

    General services management including warehousing, transportation, intermodal, order-fulfillment, dispatching, and more. Prerequisite: COMM_V 204. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  99. COMM_V 450 (3) Financial Accounting - Intermediate II

    Intermediate II. Continuation of the examination of accounting as a means of measurement and as an information system for external reporting purposes. Prerequisite: COMM_V 353 This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  100. COMM_V 452 (3) Taxes and Decision Making

    Analysis of business and financial decisions in the presence of taxes. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  101. COMM_V 453 (3) Financial Accounting-Advanced

  102. COMM_V 454 (3) Accounting for Management Control and Incentives

    Design of accounting systems for facilitating and influencing management decisions, with emphasis on performance evaluation in organizations. Prerequisite: COMM_V 294 This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  103. COMM_V 455 (3) Principles of Auditing

    Principles of internal control, audit evidence, sampling and testing; audit reports; standards; responsibilities of the external auditor. Prerequisite: COMM_V 353. Corequisite: COMM_V 450. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  104. COMM_V 456 (3) Information Technology Risks, Protection and Audit

    Business risks associated with the use of information technology. Controls for integrity, security safety, and privacy of hardware, software, data and communications. Methods for auditing information systems and their controls. Prerequisite: COMM_V 335 This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  105. COMM_V 459 (3) Financial Statement Analysis

    An examination of financial statement information from the perspective of decision makers external to the firm, e.g., investors and financial institutions. Prerequisite: COMM_V 293 and one of COMM_V 297, COMM_V 298 and one of COMM_V 370, COMM_V 397. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  106. COMM_V 460 (3) Social and Nonprofit Marketing

    Examines the role, use, and application of marketing in government agencies and nonprofit institutions. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  107. COMM_V 461 (3) Business Development

    Principles and practice of personal selling and leadership of teams: strategy, tactics, and implementation. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  108. COMM_V 462 (3) Integrated Marketing Communication

    Marketing communication campaign strategy, conceptual framework, and practical application for integrated marketing communications, including advertising. Prerequisite: COMM_V 362 This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  109. COMM_V 463 (3) Public Relations

    Managing an organization's strategic communications and relationships with diverse publics to handle issues, strengthen reputation, fulfill social responsibilities, and build trust. Topics include stakeholder engagement, issues management, crisis communications, and events and sponsorship. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  110. COMM_V 464 (3) Digital Marketing

    Investigation of Internet-based marketing, emphasizing customer-focused perspectives, competitive situations and analysis of the effectiveness of business websites using marketing principles and practices. Prerequisite: COMM_V 296. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  111. COMM_V 466 (6) New Venture Design

    Teams comprising students in COMM_V 466 and APSC_V 486 propose a new product, produce a viable prototype, and develop a business plan for the marketplace. Credit will be granted for only one of COMM_V 466, COMM_V 497, COEC_V 497, COMR_V 497, APSC_V 486, or APSC_V 496E. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  112. COMM_V 467 (3) Brand Management

    Issues and challenges commonly faced by brand managers, including: assessing brand meaning, evaluating brand health, assessing a brand's leverage potential, evaluating brand extensions, managing brands through a crisis, and assessing brand value. Prerequisite: COMM_V 296. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  113. COMM_V 468 (3) Marketing Applications

    A capstone marketing course where student teams develop comprehensive marketing plans for corporate clients by conducting industry analyses and crafting creative marketing strategies. Prerequisite: All of COMM_V 362, COMM_V 363, COMM_V 365. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  114. COMM_V 469 (3) International Marketing

    An analysis of the scope and significance of contemporary international business operations with particular reference to the marketing management problems encountered by firms with multinational branches and subsidiaries. Prerequisite: COMM_V 296. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  115. COMM_V 470 (3) Venture Capital

    Theory and evidence concerning venture capital markets. Contracting, fundraising, and financing choice covered in depth, with the aim of catering to students looking to raise money for entrepreneurial ventures or to invest in entrepreneurial ventures. Prerequisite: One of COMM_V 370, COMM_V 387. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  116. COMM_V 471 (3) Mergers and Acquisitions

    Mergers and acquisitions as a focal point in making important corporate decisions deduced from careful study of relevant business cases. Prerequisite: COMM_V 370. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  117. COMM_V 474 (3) Fixed Income Markets and Management

    Fixed income instruments and derivatives. Risk profiles and their use in establishing fixed income investment portfolios. Performance attribution techniques. Corequisite: COMM_V 374. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  118. COMM_V 475 (3) Investment Policy

    The management of security portfolios for individual and institutional investors; relation of investment policy to money markets and business fluctuations. Prerequisite: COMM_V 371. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  119. COMM_V 476 (3) Advanced Topics in Investment Management

    Applications of contemporary research, innovations, and technologies in the allocation of capital across asset classes, portfolios of assets, and individual securities. Empirical analyses of current market data using software and quantitative tools to implement potential investment and trading strategies. Prerequisite: All of COMM_V 370, COMM_V 371. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  120. COMM_V 477 (3) Risk Management and Financial Engineering

    Principles of financial engineering and risk management. Use of derivatives in risk management. Valuation and hedging models for contingent claims. Prerequisite: COMM_V 374. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  121. COMM_V 479 (3) Advanced Topics on Corporate Finance

    Corporate Finance is the process by which corporations raise money, allocate funds to real assets, and manage the flow of funds back to the investors. Topics will vary according to interests of students, instructors, and developments in the field. Corequisite: All of COMM_V 370, COMM_V 371. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  122. COMM_V 480 (3) Co-operative Work Placement III

This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  127. COMM_V 485 (3) Social Entrepreneurship

Credit will be granted for only one of COMM_V 485 or COMR_V 485. Equivalency: COMR_V 485 This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  129. COMM_V 487 (3) Environmental Management

    Explores corporate environmental strategy by introducing economic principles underlying environmental policy, law, and technologies for pollution prevention and abatement, environmental management systems, ISO-14001, energy systems, eco-marketing, and life-cycle analysis. Credit will be granted for only one of COEC_V 475, COMM_V 487, COMM_V 597, or ECON_V 371.

    Concepts and processes for the strategic management of private sector, single and multi-business unit enterprises are analysed using the case method. Methodologies which draw on economic and organizational theory are integrated to form the foundations for strategic analyses. Credit will be granted for only one of COMM_V 491 or COEC_V 491. Prerequisite: Fourth-year standing. Either (a) all of COMM_V 390, COMM_V 395 or (b) COMM_V 396. Equivalency: COEC_V 491. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  134. COMM_V 492 (3) Management Simulation

    Student teams manage hypothetical firms in a complex simulation, coordinating production, finance, marketing and strategic planning in competition with other teams. Prerequisite: Fourth-year standing. Either (a) COMM_V 390, COMM_V 395 or (b) COMM_V 396. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  135. COMM_V 495 (3) Business and Sustainable Development

  136. COMM_V 497 (3) New Enterprise Development

    The particular problems and experiences encountered in starting, developing and managing new enterprises. The course includes lectures, guest speakers, and case studies. Credit will be granted for only one of COMM_V 497, COMM_V 466, COEC_V 497, COMR_V 497, APSC_V 486, or APSC_V 496E. Prerequisite: Fourth-year standing. Either (a) all of COMM_V 390, COMM_V 395 or (b) COMM_V 396. Equivalency: COEC_V 497, COMR_V 497. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.

  137. COMM_V 498 (3) International Business Management

    Development of general environmental framework for international business studies by drawing on international and development economics, research into government-business relations and studies in comparative socio-cultural systems and political systems. Credit will be granted for only one of COMM_V 498 or COEC_V 498. Prerequisite: Either (a) all of COMM_V 390, COMM_V 395 or (b) COMM_V 396. This course is not eligible for Credit/D/Fail grading.
